## Lectern Privacy Policy

Last updated: FILL IN

### Summary

Lectern reads web page text aloud using voices already installed on your device. In its
default configuration, Lectern makes no network requests. The text it reads never leaves
your browser. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics.

### What Lectern accesses

- **Page text you ask it to read.** When you invoke a read action, Lectern reads text from
  the active tab in order to pass it to your device's speech engine. This happens entirely
  on your device. The text is not transmitted, logged, or stored.
- **Your preferences.** Voice selection, speech rate, pitch, and mode settings are stored
  locally in browser storage. They are not synced to any server operated by Proctorio.

### What Lectern does not do

- Does not create an account or require sign-in.
- Does not collect analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports.
- Does not transmit page content anywhere.
- Does not store the content it reads.
- Does not track browsing history.
- Does not sell or share data with third parties, because it does not collect any.

### Exam-safe mode

When exam-safe mode is active, Lectern uses only local device voices, disables any
network-capable feature regardless of user setting, and reads only the active tab.

### Children's privacy

Lectern is used in K-12 and higher education settings. Because Lectern collects no
personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children. Address
FERPA, COPPA, and the NY Parents' Bill of Rights posture here if institutional
